Atrium — Brand palette & type

Cream, ink,
lime & amber.

Single source of truth: packages/ui/src/theme.css — a Tailwind @theme block, so every token is also a utility (bg-lime, text-charcoal). Warm cream ground, deep teal ink, lime as the one bright, amber as the accent. Terracotta for state — never a traffic-light red.

Text & line

Departure from the sheet
The sheet sets muted at 55% — that measures 3.55:1 on cream and cannot carry body text. Ours is 66%: 4.94:1, clearing AA. It is the one value on the list that is not the sheet's.

Type — three faces, no more

Declared once in theme.css and loaded by the Next font loader. Headings are set in the sans; the serif appears only inside an <em>. That single rule is the brand's voice on the page.

Aa Bb 123
Inter Tight — font-sans
Everything by default: headings (weight 400, tracking −0.035em), body, UI, numerals. 78 heading uses across the apps.
Aa Bb 123
Instrument Serif italic — font-serif
The emphasis face, never a whole heading. :where(h1,h2,h3,h4) em switches to it automatically — write <em> and the type system does the rest.
Aa Bb
Nothing You Could Do — font-script
In reserve. Only ScriptAccent in @atrium/ui/legacy uses it — 3 call sites. Do not reach for it in new work.

Traffic that turns into tables

The sans carries the sentence and the serif italic carries the promise inside it. Body copy caps at 72ch, sets text-wrap: pretty, and tracks −0.008em; headings use text-wrap: balance. Components opt into a role rather than picking a size.

The teal/mint sheet is retired
tokens/tokens.css still ships the old Deep Teal + Mint ramp, but compat.css repoints all 21 raw values at the ones above — --mint-400 now resolves to lime, --cloud-200 to cream. It is scaffolding with an expiry date: author against theme.css only, and delete a line from compat.css each time a surface migrates.

Rules of use

DO — use the Tailwind utility the theme generates (bg-lime, text-charcoal, border-line). Set text in charcoal / body / muted; keep lime for one bright moment per view; write <em> for emphasis and let the serif rule fire; import Button, Tag, Card from @atrium/ui rather than restyling a fill.
DON'T — no new hex in a component, no muted-soft under text (2.81:1), no traffic-light red for errors (terracotta only), no whole heading in the serif, no editorial accent as a component default, and nothing authored against the old --mint-* / --cloud-* names.
